# Evolution Cycle

## Ideal State

Deliver an evolved portfolio of hard-to-vary explanations, not a brainstorm: each survivor has a traceable lineage, distinct causal hypothesis, explicit relation to the target phenomenon, and clear test status.

## Full Cycle

1. **CONSUME** — extract observations, anomalies, mechanisms, constraints, and serious rivals.
2. **DREAM** — generate strange but structured causal hypotheses; label fantasy, hypothesis, and evidence separately.
3. **DAYDREAM** — apply counterfactuals, role reversals, scale changes, and causal leaps.
4. **SALON** — collide independent disciplinary lenses without premature consensus.
5. **STEAL** — transfer mechanisms across domains deliberately imperfectly; state the useful mismatch.
6. **MATE** — recombine causal families and mutate assumptions, dependencies, scale, or time horizon; record lineage.
7. **TEST** — challenge candidates against observations, constraints, evidence, and feasible interventions.
8. **EXPLANATORY SELECTION** — apply ExplanatorySelection.md and HardToVary.md. Demote candidates that merely fit, relabel, or decorate the phenomenon. Advance only constrained causal cores with necessary parts, distinctive counterfactuals, and risky discriminators.
9. **SELECT** — retain a diverse portfolio of hard-to-vary explanatory survivors plus a controlled high-upside wildcard; preserve useful mechanisms from rejected lineages.
10. **EVOLVE** — seed the next population from survivors and useful rejected mechanisms.
11. **META-LEARN** — update disciplines, mutations, tests, and selection rules from what survived and what was falsely attractive.

## Output Contract

Return the phenomenon and evidence boundary; survivor portfolio with causal core, lineage, necessary components, hard-to-vary tests, and next experiment; wildcards and retired mechanisms; evolution report; and revised selection rule.

## Stop Conditions

Stop when the cycle budget is exhausted, evidence decisively favors a portfolio, constraints rule out candidates, or marginal explanatory learning collapses. State the stopping condition and what would justify resuming.
